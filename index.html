What it finds

One scan, every kind of clutter

Each category is something the app actually detects on your device — no guesswork, no fake numbers.

Duplicate & similar

Perceptual hashing and Vision feature prints group identical and near-identical shots so you can keep one and clear the rest.

Blurry photos

Sharpness scoring surfaces the genuinely out-of-focus shots — pre-selecting only the very blurry, never your keepers.

Old screenshots

Finds screenshots by type and age. Receipts and financial captures are never pre-selected for deletion.

Large & heavy videos

Sorts big, 4K, slo-mo, time-lapse, and ProRes videos so the real space-hogs are easy to find.

Compress videos

Shrink large videos with Light, Balanced, or Maximum modes. The original is kept until the compressed copy is verified.

AI best-photo pick

For each group, on-device scoring recommends the sharpest, best-exposed shot to keep — you always have the final say.

FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. All scanning and AI analysis happen entirely on your device. Your photos, videos, and the analysis derived from them never leave your iPhone or iPad and are never sent to us or anyone else.
Will it delete photos automatically?
Never. The app only recommends. You review and select what to remove, and both the app and iOS ask you to confirm. Favorites, hidden photos, and items from the last 24 hours are never pre-selected.
What can CleanSnap AI find?
Duplicate and visually similar photos, blurry shots, old screenshots, bursts, Live Photos, RAW photos, and large or heavy videos (4K, slo-mo, ProRes). It can also compress videos to save space.
My storage didn't drop after deleting. Why?
Deleted items move to Photos → Recently Deleted for up to about 30 days before iOS frees the space. To recover storage immediately, open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted → Delete All.
Is it really free?
Yes. The scan, preview, and review are free, and you can clean up to 20 items and compress one video at no cost. Premium unlocks unlimited cleanup and compression plus AI best-photo selection and batch tools.
