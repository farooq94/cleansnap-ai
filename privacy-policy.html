CleanSnap AI does not collect, transmit, or store your photos, videos, thumbnails, or any media content. On-device analytics collect only anonymous usage events (counts, not content). See /docs/compliance/ for precise definitions.

Anonymous analytics are used to understand feature usage and improve the app. No personal data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. CleanSnap AI does not receive or store payment card information. Subscription management is through your Apple ID.
